\caption{\textbf{The forecasting family: 12 latent-structure worlds, 8 training and 4 held-out.}
Each world is a small linear-Gaussian DAG in which observed news $x$ drives one or more latent
opinions $o$ that emit an observed poll $p$. Every opinion is \emph{dynamic}---it carries over and
mean-reverts week to week (gray $\phi$ self-loops; unrolled over two weeks in
Fig.~\ref{fig:exp3-unroll})---so forecasting next week's poll means tracking a \emph{moving} latent,
not just reading the current news. In each world a single edge, the
\textcolor{teal!60!black}{hidden gain $g$} (dashed, boxed), is drawn once per city and must be
inferred from the news/poll history alone; everything else about the structure is disclosed in the
prompt. The family varies what surrounds that edge: chain depth and delay (Chain-2/3/4, Lagged),
parallel and opposed pathways (Echo, Inhibitory, Mediators, Two-news split), coupled feedback
(Two-news feedback), and which edge is hidden (an input gain, an inter-latent coupling, or the
persistence itself in the hidden-$\phi$ worlds). The four \textcolor{orange!75!black}{held-out
worlds} (bottom)---including \emph{Direct}, the Experiment-2 world---are never trained on. The
shared task is always to forecast the probed poll; a per-structure Kalman filter that marginalizes
$g$ is the oracle. The figure is generated from the world matrices themselves.}
\label{fig:exp3-family}
\end{figure*}
