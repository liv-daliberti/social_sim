\section{Related Work}
\label{related_work}

\textbf{Judgemental and language-model forecasting.}
Research on judgemental forecasting separates accuracy from process. Strong human
forecasters use base rates, decompose questions, seek diverse evidence, and update
frequently; unaided expert judgment and manual adjustments can remain
miscalibrated or biased
\citep{tetlock2005expert,mellers2014psychological,tetlock2016superforecasting,lawrence2006judgmental}.
Autocast and ForecastBench provide time-indexed, leakage-controlled evaluations of
language models \citep{zou2022forecasting,karger2025forecastbench}. Retrieval-augmented
generation, aggregation, and ensembling have brought some systems close to crowd
accuracy \citep{lewis2020retrieval,halawi2024approaching,schoenegger2024wisdom},
while tournament entries report more mixed real-world performance
\citep{schoenegger2023prediction}. These studies measure forecast
quality; we test how forecasts respond to evidence, examples, and training.

\textbf{In-context inference and analogy.}
Transformers can infer unseen functions from input--output examples, with success
depending on the training distribution
\citep{garg2022what,chan2022data}. Language models also solve some abstract
analogy tasks, and analogical prompting can improve reasoning
\citep{webb2023emergent,yasunaga2024large}. Because either relationship inference or
analogical matching could explain Coin City performance, we compare model forecasts
with a deterministic, context-conditioned analogical regression.

\textbf{World structure and behavioral evidence.}
World models represent environmental dynamics for prediction or control
\citep{ha2018world,lecun2022path}, and sequence models can encode task state and
support predictions about agents \citep{li2023emergent,andreas2022agent}. Predictive
success, however, can rest on shortcut features and need not indicate
understanding or support a new task or structural change
\citep{geirhos2020shortcut,mitchell2023ai,vafa2025foundation}. Causal formalisms
make such structure explicit \citep{pearl2009causality,peters2017elements}, and
formal benchmarks test queries over specified graphs \citep{jin2023cladder}; we test whether forecasts behave as if a model inferred
and reused predictive relationships when no graph label is requested. Because
natural-language rationales may not faithfully describe the computation behind an
answer \citep{jacovi2020towards,turpin2023language}, we use behavioral interventions
that specify expected invariances or directional changes
\citep{ribeiro2020beyond,elazar2021measuring}.
