\subsection*{AI use statement}

Generative AI systems are central objects of study in this work and produced the
forecasts and experimental responses that we evaluate. We also used generative AI
tools to generate candidate counterfactual and synthetic materials; refine the
conceptual framing, hypotheses, methodology, and experimental design; implement,
debug, clean, and reformat code and data; support quantitative analysis and result
interpretation; create and edit figures; and draft, edit, structure, and format this
manuscript. No formal mathematical theorem or proof is advanced in this work, and
translation and qualitative or thematic data analysis were not applicable.
AI-generated experimental records were retained for audit and subjected to the
programmatic validity checks described in the appendices; AI-assisted code, text,
figures, analyses, and claims were reviewed by the authors. The authors take
responsibility for the final content of this work, including text, claims, and
artifacts produced with the aid of generative AI.

\subsection*{Ethics statement}

The reported analyses use public market metadata and price histories, model
outputs, synthetic worlds, and an unpaid adult review of synthetic materials. The
review collected no identifying or sensitive information. Under our institutional
guidelines, it does not constitute human-subjects research; consequently, no IRB
review was sought. Counterfactual evidence packets were evaluated offline and were
not posted to markets or used for trading. We report aggregate model behavior
rather than recommend deployment.

\subsection*{Reproducibility statement}

Appendices~A--C specify the prompts, data-generating processes, sample construction,
censoring, estimators, deployment settings, missingness, hyperparameters, frozen
baselines, and fail-closed checks. Builders, manifests, checksums, and saved
generations support each reported result. Experiment~3's Qwen3-4B Coin City
comparison is complete at the registered round 300 under greedy and five-draw
stochastic decoding; the Qwen3-8B and Llama-3.1-8B grids were incomplete when this
analysis was finalized. The completed Qwen3-4B arm-by-disclosure factorial is reported
as a complementary structural-transfer study in the appendix.
The historical test was evaluated only after all three seeds completed, without
test feedback during training or model selection.
