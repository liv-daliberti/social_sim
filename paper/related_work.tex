\section{Related Work}
\label{related_work}

\textbf{Judgmental and language-model forecasting.}
Judgmental forecasting distinguishes accuracy from process. Strong forecasters use
base rates, decompose questions, seek diverse evidence, and update often, whereas
unaided expert judgment and manual adjustments can remain miscalibrated or biased
\citep{tetlock2005expert,mellers2014psychological,tetlock2016superforecasting,lawrence2006judgmental}.
Autocast and ForecastBench provide time-indexed, leakage-controlled evaluations of
language models \citep{zou2022forecasting,karger2025forecastbench}. Retrieval,
aggregation, and ensembling bring some systems close to crowd accuracy
\citep{lewis2020retrieval,halawi2024approaching,schoenegger2024wisdom}, while
tournament entries report mixed real-world performance
\citep{schoenegger2023prediction}. These studies establish forecasting
performance; we test whether that performance reflects the selection, revision,
and transfer of predictive relationships.

\textbf{Induction, in-context inference, and analogy.}
Inductive inference selects predictive relationships from prior knowledge, context,
and sparse observations, then revises them as evidence accumulates
\citep{tenenbaum2006theory,griffiths2010probabilistic}. Transformers infer unseen
functions from input--output examples, with success depending on the training
distribution \citep{garg2022what,chan2022data}; language models also solve some
abstract analogy tasks or benefit from analogical prompting
\citep{webb2023emergent,yasunaga2024large}. Coin City tests the same sequence---select
the cue-matched reference, estimate its relationship, apply it to a new case---which a
deterministic analogical regression instantiates. It differs from in-context
function fitting in what the target supplies: at $k=0$ City C has no rows, so
there is nothing to regress on, and the relationship has to be taken from another
city under a linguistic cue. The arbitrary-label arm additionally reverses the
label-to-regime mapping across episodes, so no fixed prior over the label words
suffices.


\textbf{World structure and behavioral evidence.}
World models encode environmental dynamics, while sequence models can encode task
state and support predictions about agents
\citep{ha2018world,lecun2022path,li2023emergent,andreas2022agent}. Yet shortcut-based
success can fail under task or structural shift
\citep{geirhos2020shortcut,mitchell2023ai,vafa2025foundation}. Causal formalisms and
graph benchmarks specify structure explicitly
\citep{pearl2009causality,peters2017elements,jin2023cladder}. We instead test reuse of
predictive relationships without a graph label, using interventions with predicted
invariances or directional changes \citep{ribeiro2020beyond,elazar2021measuring}
rather than relying on potentially unfaithful rationales
\citep{jacovi2020towards,turpin2023language}.
