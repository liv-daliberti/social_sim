\section{Limitations and Conclusion}
\label{sec:limitations}
\label{sec:conclusion}

The evidence supports behavioral relationship use, not a general internal world
model. Experiment~1's packet arms differ in wording, and its relationships may be
familiar. Coin City tests two regimes in one synthetic world; its alternate
population changes separation and dispersion together. Inference stacks differ,
transfer uses three seeds, and pretraining exposure is unauditable. Unreliable
cues, new variables, longer horizons, other recipes, and crowd superiority remain
open.

Across experiments, forecasts behave as if selecting context-dependent
relationships: cues reverse selection, target evidence overrides them, and
episode-local examples induce arbitrary mappings. Probes decode this mapping where
behavior recovers it; a targeted patch changes one checkpoint's forecast; and
episode-matched training partly transfers use under joint shift. \mbox{Market-beating
alpha lies outside the claim}: historical training lowers base error on sealed
families; crowd differences remain unresolved.
