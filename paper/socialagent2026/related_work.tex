\section{From plausible behavior to relational validity}
\label{sec:related-work}

\paragraph{Social agents and simulation validity.}
Generative agents can sustain believable individual and collective behavior in
interactive environments \citep{park2023generative}, and LMs can reproduce
selected patterns from human-subject experiments or demographic samples
\citep{aher2023simulate,argyle2023out}. Yet matching a marginal distribution is
not the same as preserving the conditional structure of a population. Synthetic
survey responses can resemble human averages while understating variation,
changing under small prompt or model updates, and yielding different regression
relationships \citep{bisbee2024synthetic}. This distinction motivates our unit of
analysis. We do not ask whether an LM is a faithful proxy for a person or
population. We ask whether its \emph{forecast changes for the right structural
reason} under paired interventions.

\paragraph{Judgmental forecasting.}
Strong human forecasters combine base rates, decomposition, diverse evidence,
and repeated revision \citep{tetlock2005expert,mellers2014psychological,tetlock2016superforecasting}.
Forecasting benchmarks increasingly evaluate LMs on time-indexed or
leakage-controlled questions \citep{zou2022forecasting,karger2025forecastbench};
retrieval and ensembling bring some systems near crowd accuracy
\citep{halawi2024approaching,schoenegger2024wisdom}. These results establish what
probabilities models produce. Our focus is the relationship behind the update:
whether evidence is treated as probative, which contextual analogue is selected,
and whether that choice survives a change in domain or mechanism.

\paragraph{Induction and behavioral evidence.}
Transformers can infer unseen functions from input--output examples, conditional
on their training distribution \citep{garg2022what,chan2022data}; LMs also
solve some abstract analogy tasks \citep{webb2023emergent,yasunaga2024large}.
Our selection tests differ in what the target supplies: it displays no examples of
its own, so the relationship must be carried from a demonstrated analogue under a
qualitative cue, and an arbitrary-label variant reverses that cue's mapping across
episodes.
World-model accounts emphasize representations of environmental state or
dynamics \citep{ha2018world,lecun2022path,li2023emergent}, while shortcut learning
warns that predictive success can fail under structural shift
\citep{geirhos2020shortcut,mitchell2023ai,vafa2025foundation}. We connect
these literatures through paired behavioral tests. Each intervention has a
predicted change or invariance, and later stages remove explanations based on
familiar semantics, answer distributions, or surface domain.
