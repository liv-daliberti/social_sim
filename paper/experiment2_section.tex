\subsection{Experiment 2: Context-Conditioned Relationship Selection}
\label{sec:exp2}

Experiment~2 asks which numerical relationship a model applies to a new target.
Two reference cities demonstrate a strong and a weak response regime; a one-sentence
cue assigns target City C to one of them; and City C's own cases accumulate from
$k=0$ to $k=4$. Arms are matched on every displayed number, so a contrast isolates
the cue alone. The reference sentences state which regime responds more strongly,
so the semantic arms measure how a stated assignment becomes a number rather than
whether the assignment can be learned. An arbitrary-label arm, whose
\texttt{KIV}/\texttt{ZOR} mapping carries no stated direction and reverses across
episodes, carries the induction claim. Full prompts, per-arm results and analysis
details are in Appendix~\ref{app:coin-city}.

\textbf{Selection and revision.} With no City C observations, the cue lowers
forecast error for every deployment, by $0.37$ to $0.92$ poll points with all six
bootstrap intervals excluding zero; the cross-model median falls from 2.59 to 1.85
and forecast-implied responses correlate $.52$--$.70$ with City C's true
responsiveness against $-.06$ to $.08$ without the cue. Every deployment also
matches or beats a context-blind OLS benchmark fitted on all displayed rows
(Appendix Table~\ref{tab:coin-city-vs-ols}).
Naming the opposite regime reverses the assignment rather than degrading it:
error rises to $4.20$--$4.36$, $1.52$--$1.76$ points above no context, and
correlations invert to $-.50$--$-.68$. Both effects are transient. By $k=4$ the
cue advantage narrows to $0.12$--$0.23$ points and the wrong-label penalty to
$-0.01$--$+0.38$, so target observations displace the cue once they disagree with it.

\textbf{The cue selects a displayed reference, not a prior on its words.} Because
the reference sentences name the direction, a model could reach these numbers from
a prior about news coverage alone. Partialling out the regime separates the
accounts: a fit to four noisy reference cases carries sampling error
($\mathrm{SD}=.332$ against a generator $.03$) that a cue-determined prior cannot
reproduce. Four arms make four predictions and all four hold at $k=0$. With no
reference cases displayed, neither reference loads. With both displayed and no
cue, they load equally---the model pools. The correct cue concentrates loading on
the named reference ($.51$--$.86$) and removes it from the other
($.04$--$.10$), and inversion moves it wholesale onto the reference the false cue
names ($.61$--$.90$). This also explains why implied-slope correlations plateau
near $.66$ rather than $.996$: transferring a noisy reference estimate is capped
at that reference's own correlation with the truth
(Appendix~\ref{app:coin-city-reference-selection}).

\textbf{Induction of a novel mapping.} The arbitrary-label mapping must be
inferred from the reference examples within each episode. At $k=0$, twelve of
fifteen systems recover it, including all five hosted ones; within Qwen3 the
recovery interval excludes zero only from 14B upward and within Llama only at 70B,
so the transition differs by family and is not monotone in parameter count.
Hidden states carry the induced mapping, weakly and only after computation.
Probes on the semantic arm reach AUC $1.000$ but select the first transformer
block, and mean-pooled input embeddings already reach $1.000$: that result is
lexical. Under arbitrary labels the embedding control falls to $.555$ and held-out
AUC is $.678$ at block 26 of 40 for Qwen3-14B and $.778$ at block 50 of 64 for
Qwen2.5-32B, while Llama~3.1-8B---the checkpoint whose forecasts do not recover the
mapping---is null in all four conditions. Patching those states at the two City C
label tokens shifts Qwen3-14B's regime-aligned forecast by $.333$ at $k=0$
($p<.0001$) and does nothing at $k=4$, so the induced mapping causes the zero-shot
forecast and direct evidence displaces it
(Appendix~\ref{app:coin-city-mechanistic-probe}).

Context therefore selects which of two demonstrated relationships a model applies,
that selection reaches a particular displayed reference rather than a remembered
association, and target evidence overrides it. Three things bound the reading. The
regimes are far apart and the cue is binary, so selection here is a two-way choice
rather than a graded one. The exact within-regime coefficient is never recovered:
residual-slope decoding fails in every model--$k$ cell. And the mechanistic
evidence covers three open checkpoints, with the causal test on one; the hosted
deployments are behavioral throughout. Experiment~3 tests transfer after
episode-matched training.
