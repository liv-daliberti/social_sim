\subsection{Experiment 2: Context-Conditioned Relationship Selection}
\label{sec:exp2}

Experiment~2 asks which numerical relationship applies. Reference cases define a
qualitative cue linking a new target to one of two response regimes; matched
correct- and opposite-context prompts and accumulating target observations test
selection and subsequent revision. Full prompts, analysis details, and per-arm
results appear in Appendix~\ref{app:coin-city}.

\textbf{Correct-context contrast.}
With no City C observations ($k=0$), adding its context lowers MAE relative to the
matched no-context arm for every deployment. Improvements range from $0.37$ to
$0.92$ poll points, with all bootstrap intervals excluding zero; the cross-model
median falls from 2.59 to 1.85. All numeric inputs are identical in the two arms.
Forecast-implied responses correlate $.52$--$.70$ with City C's true responsiveness,
compared with $-.06$ to $.08$ without context. By $k=4$, the MAE advantage narrows
to $0.12$--$0.23$ because City C's own cases reveal the relationship, which also
erases a $k=0$ advantage over a context-blind OLS benchmark
(Appendix Table~\ref{tab:coin-city-vs-ols}).

\textbf{Opposite-context contrast.}
At $k=0$, the opposite label raises MAE to $4.20$--$4.36$, $1.52$--$1.76$ points
above no context, and response correlations become $-.50$ to $-.68$. As City C
observations accumulate, the wrong-label penalty falls to $-0.01$--$+0.38$ points
at $k=4$. Models therefore use the cue when evidence is sparse and reduce its
influence when observations disagree.

\textbf{Models infer a new relationship from demonstrations and apply it to a
target.}
The arbitrary-label mapping reverses across episodes and must be inferred from
their examples. At $k=0$, Qwen3-14B and Qwen3-32B, all four Qwen2.5 checkpoints,
Llama~3.1-70B, and all five hosted systems recover it. Within Qwen3 the recovery
interval excludes zero only from 14B upward and within Llama only at 70B, but the
transition differs by family and is not monotone in parameter count. Kimi, both
Opus deployments, and GPT also lower forecast error, separating induction from
numerical calibration (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:coin-city-symbol-context}).

\textbf{Forecasts track the cue-named reference, not a lexical prior.}
The reference sentences state which regime responds more strongly, so the semantic
contrast alone cannot exclude a prior attached to those words. Partialling out the
regime separates the accounts, because a fit to four noisy reference cases carries
sampling error ($\mathrm{SD}=.332$ against a generator $.03$) that a
cue-determined prior cannot reproduce. Four arms make four predictions and all
four hold at $k=0$: with no reference cases displayed neither reference loads;
with both displayed and no City C label the two load equally, the pooling
signature; the correct label concentrates loading on the named reference
($.51$--$.86$) and removes it from the other ($.04$--$.10$); and inversion moves
the loading wholesale onto the reference the false cue names ($.61$--$.90$). This
also explains why implied-slope correlations plateau near $.66$ rather than
$.996$: faithful transfer of a noisy reference estimate is capped at that
reference's own correlation with the truth. Under arbitrary symbols the same test
marks twelve of fifteen systems, agreeing with the $\Delta\rho$ contrast on all
fifteen (Appendix~\ref{app:coin-city-reference-selection}).

\textbf{Hidden states carry the induced relationship, weakly.}
Linear probes decode the cue-selected regime across six open checkpoints spanning
Qwen and Llama (AUC $.844$--$1.000$), reversing onto the stated relationship under
inversion. That result is largely lexical, and we treat it as such: mean-pooled
input embeddings alone reach AUC $1.000$, and the probe selects the first
transformer block. Replacing the words with arbitrary labels and enlarging the
sealed test to 88 episodes changes both. The embedding control collapses to
$.555$, the selected layer moves from the first block to block 26 of 40, and
held-out regime AUC is $.678$ at $k=0$ ($p=.0016$). Two further checkpoints at the
same 88 episodes complete the pattern: Qwen2.5-32B reaches $.778$ ($p<.001$) at
layer 50 of 64, while Llama~3.1-8B is null in all four conditions
($p=.25$--$.89$)---and Llama~3.1-8B is exactly the checkpoint whose forecasts do
not recover the mapping. Decodability tracks behavioral recovery rather than
appearing wherever the probe is applied. Slope $R^2$ is $.016$ and does not reject
its null, so an induced response family is linearly represented after computation
while the exact coefficient is not
(Figure~\ref{fig:coin-city-mechanistic-main};
Appendix~\ref{app:coin-city-mechanistic-probe}).

\textbf{The induced relationship causally controls the zero-shot forecast.}
Decodability is not mediation, so we intervene. Under arbitrary labels an
episode's correct and swapped prompts are byte-identical through the City C label,
leaving exactly two differing token positions. Replacing the residual stream at
those two positions with the opposite arm's states shifts Qwen3-14B's
regime-aligned forecast by $.333$ at $k=0$ against a sign-flip null of
$[-.177,.178]$ ($p<.0001$), with $347/347$ exact self-patch controls and all eight
checks of a stability gate frozen before the result. With four City C cases every
patch site is null ($p\ge.64$): direct evidence displaces the induced label
causally, as it does behaviorally. This is one checkpoint under arbitrary labels
(Appendix~\ref{app:coin-city-causal-patch}).

The cue-reversal and evidence-accumulation contrasts test reference selection,
response application, and revision; the reference-selection and patching analyses
show the selection is of a particular displayed reference and, in one checkpoint,
that it causes the forecast. Experiment~3 tests transfer after episode-matched
training.
