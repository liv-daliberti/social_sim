# Fresh finite-mechanism evaluation: frozen protocol

## Scope and prospective amendment (22 September 2026)

The original 80-concept qualitative blueprint is superseded by 80 fully specified
finite-rule instances: ten parameterized instances in each of eight mechanism
classes (calibration, prerequisite timing, component quorums, procedural waivers,
proportional brokerage, directed reachability, bounded two-step feedback, and
report dependence). The outcome wrapper is shared. These are **eight authored
mechanism classes, not 80 independent mechanisms or representative natural news**.
They provide a controlled test of relational updating, with exact normative signs
and probabilities, rather than validate all of the original prospective concepts.
Each source concept has a recorded disposition in the candidate ledger. The
original development results remain exploratory and are not pooled with these data.

Candidate v1 used excessively similar scalar-threshold rules. Its two screening
jobs were cancelled while pending, before any responses. Candidate v2 introduced
distinct rules. Candidate v3 clarified conditioning in calibration/dependence
cases and the distinction between direct audit information and its implications.
Candidate v4 additionally repairs the scope of independence in the paraphrase and distinguishes the local procedural approval coin from the final inspection. All versions are retained. These revisions preceded target-model responses.

## Materials and uncertainty

Every instance has three separately elicited contexts. The news is literally
identical across them; only bindings between named actors and the ordinary,
cancellation, and archive channels change. Every channel description appears in
every context. Word and punctuation multisets match exactly within each variant.
Question orientation, rising/falling news, reported actor and role row order are
counterbalanced prospectively; the balance audit records actual counts, including
unavoidable imbalance in strata of ten.

Each channel has a binary latent variable X. An audit changes its stated chance
from 1/4 to 3/4 or conversely, without revealing X. All other conditional rules
remain fixed. Calibration/dependence cases explicitly condition the X probability
on stored reports already, avoiding double conditioning. The channel firing rate
is an exact mixture of two finite enumerations or Bayesian likelihood ratios.
The project requires inspection success (4/5) and either reserve-team success
(1/5) or ordinary-channel firing without cancellation-channel firing. Independence
is stated. Thus P(completion)=4/5*[1/5+4/5*a*(1-b)]; both outcomes remain possible
under all contexts and updates. The probability of the question's outcome is
never supplied as the model's prior: each model elicits its own context baseline.

The same family has base, name-only, paraphrase, and same-text resample variants.
The last differs only in request seed; it measures ordinary sampling discrepancy.
There are 80 parent instances, 320 material variants and 960 context units. The
numeric task has one baseline and three independent branches (new, no, repeated
news), hence 3,840 planned records per reasoning mode. Controls use identical
instructions and the repeated fact is present verbatim at baseline.

## Screening and retention

No new human screening is requested or claimed. Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct and
Qwen3-14B screen all 720 distinct packets (80 x 3 wordings x 3 contexts), in
opaque-ID order, without labels, sibling contexts, author derivations, numerical
target outputs, or target performance. Same-text resamples have exact duplicate
visible packets and do not require duplicate screening. The originally suggested
Mistral cache lacks weights; this is why the available Llama checkpoint is used.
Qwen3-14B shares a model family with the Qwen3-32B target, so reviewer independence
is limited. Review is screening, not human validation or a measure of accuracy.

Reviewers judge direction, whether both outcomes remain possible, and ambiguity.
All raw outputs and parse failures are retained. Every flagged concern is
adjudicated against the explicit rules and separate mathematical audit. Model
agreement is not a filter; a wrong directional judgment alone never drops a case.
Material defects require a versioned repair and verification before freezing.
No unresolved material defect may be relabeled as passed merely to start a run. The v3 nonthinking screens had poor directional agreement (228/720 and 179/720 planned packets); these are disclosed as weak screening rather than validation. A partial Qwen3-14B thinking screen is retained separately (32 attempts, 11 truncated). The final v5 screen uses both checkpoints without thinking: Llama agrees on 224/720 planned packets (676 valid), and Qwen14 on 182/720 (720 valid). All valid endpoint judgments retain both outcomes. These weak screens do not validate labels; explicit-rule calculation and disclosed author adjudication determine the labels. All 80 instances are retained.

## Targets and matched comparison

Primary fresh local comparison: the identical cached Qwen3-32B checkpoint with
thinking enabled versus disabled. Same visible prompts, own-context baselines,
request seeds, temperature 0.7, top-p 1, one response, unconstrained decoding,
8,192 maximum output tokens, 16,384 context tokens, batch size 16, eager execution,
2-way tensor parallelism on A6000 GPUs. The effective thinking chat prefix is the intended
manipulation; hardware and software are recorded. A single optional Markdown JSON
fence is accepted in both arms. Enabled reasoning must close before the final
probability. Truncations and all received invalid outputs are terminal failures;
there is no resampling to obtain valid answers. This is a matched deployment
comparison, not proof that visible thought alone causes any difference.

The earlier Qwen2.5-72B/Llama-70B development results remain separate. The fresh
primary comparison focuses compute on the missing reasoning-by-wording design.
The separate old direction-only companion is not treated as fresh evidence.

Frontier: one GPT-5.6 Sol low-reasoning evaluation on a prospectively selected
24-parent subset, three per mechanism class, base and paraphrase variants only.
This is 48 variants, 144 context units, 576 planned records, not 48 independent
families. It shares the local prompts, priors procedure, controls and scoring.
The subset is fixed before any target output. Use a 2,048 output-token allowance,
strict probability JSON, zero SDK retries, staged Batch baseline/update submission,
and the existing durable authorization ledger. The operational cap is $24 within
the user's $25 total authorization. Unknown billed requests retain reservation.
No repeated frontier development or spending of released funds on extra cases.

## Scoring fixed before collection

Primary outcome: both signed news updates strictly correct for a parent instance,
reported separately by wording and mode; a zero update is a failure. Primary
reasoning contrast: enabled minus disabled paired-reversal rate on base wording.
Report the same contrast for each wording and the reasoning-by-wording difference
in contrasts (paraphrase minus base; name-only minus base). Report unconditional
sign accuracy, baseline endpoint blocking, final-output failures, and all planned
denominators. Invalid/missing responses count as binary failures. No endpoint
conditioning or successful-movement-only scoring is permitted.

Report signed and absolute broken-link movement and the existing prespecified
2 percentage-point criterion: the 90% signed-mean interval must lie strictly within
[-2,2] and the upper 95% mean-absolute interval must be below 2. Any missing planned
broken measurement makes this criterion indeterminate. Report no-news and repeated
news drift alongside it, plus control-corrected signed effects. Failure to reject
zero drift does not demonstrate equivalence.

For controlled edits report update discrepancy versus base, joint correctness,
and discrepancy in excess of the same-text resample reference. Similar answers
alone do not establish correctness; confidence intervals crossing zero do not
establish wording equivalence. Report oracle absolute probability and update error
as secondary descriptive diagnostics, including effects smaller than 2 points.
The finite materials intentionally include small but nonzero normative effects. Prespecified descriptive sign-accuracy bins are absolute oracle changes below 1 point, 1 to below 2 points, and at least 2 points; all remain in the primary denominator. Candidate v5 explicitly states primitive-draw independence except where a rule specifies dependence or conditioning on stored observations.

All intervals resample entire parent instances, keeping contexts, wordings and
modes together, with 2,000 draws, fixed seed 20260922. They are descriptive for
this authored corpus. Additionally report each mechanism class, equal-class macro
averages and leave-one-class-out reasoning contrasts; repeated parameterizations
are not independent evidence for broad mechanism generalization. GPT comparisons
use only the same 24 local parent instances and wordings, and are descriptive
because its reasoning allowance and deployment differ.

## Freeze and publication gates

Before any fresh target inference, finalize screening/adjudication; exact materials,
label key, controls, scorer, model checkpoint/configuration, subset, exclusions,
code hashes and this protocol enter an immutable design manifest. Complete an
explicit check against all older evaluated plans. The frontier generation freeze
may add provider token-count receipts without changing this design. All numeric
outputs retain raw completions and provenance; audit own-prior association, parsing,
planned records and scores before integrating results in the ICLR appendix.
Paper prose must distinguish development, model-screened finite-rule evaluation,
and the smaller frontier subset. No uncollected result is written as a finding.
