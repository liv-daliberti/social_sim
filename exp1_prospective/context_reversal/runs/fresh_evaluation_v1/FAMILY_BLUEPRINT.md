# Prospective fresh-family authorship blueprint

Status: **80 candidate concepts, not evaluation materials or a frozen cohort**.
This document proposes eight strata with ten candidate IDs each. It contains no
final prompts, target-model responses, screened items, or frozen label key.
A distinct ID, setting, or noun does not establish a distinct mechanism or an
independent observation. Structural novelty and material adequacy must be checked
before these concepts can become an evaluation cohort. The finite authored cohort,
with dependence within mechanism strata, remains the intended scope of inference.

## What is already represented

| Original mechanism class | Families | Existing pathways to avoid merely renaming |
|---|---:|---|
| Competitive allocation | 7 | Campaign effort, proposal engineering, bid cost, assembly races, sports practice, school qualification, book promotion |
| Buffer flow | 7 | Carton, water, resin, soil moisture, sediment, filtration and stage-space stocks |
| Biological interaction | 3 | Microbial secretion, predation and plant damage |
| Queue load | 2 | Computing backlogs and appointment processing |
| Signal interaction | 1 | Physical constructive/destructive radio interference |

The proposed strata below emphasize additional graph structures, decision rules,
and information relationships. Using a familiar domain is permissible only when
the intervention, mediator and outcome pathway change substantively. In particular,
measurement reliability is different from physical wave interference; procedural
eligibility is different from raising a competitor's performance; intermediary
allocation requires an explicit allocation rule beyond assigning resources directly
to a beneficiary. Flag overlaps with the old set and overlaps between new candidates.

## Common authorship contract

- Each eventual family has one uncertain YES outcome, one newly observed message,
  and supporting, opposing and broken-connection contexts. The message is identical
  across those contexts. This blueprint names possibilities; no per-item direction
  label is being certified here.
- Describe every relevant pathway before authoring a direction key. Specify a
  beneficial route, an adverse route and a separate route in **every** context;
  change the binding between the reported actor/channel and these roles. The null
  role has no path to the target under the stated premises, including no shared
  budget, timing, feedback or information channel. Do not add a unique word such as
  “unrelated” only to the broken context.
- Preserve the exact lowercased word multiset across the three relationship
  contexts, including names, quantities, role descriptions and qualifications.
  Permuting actor-to-role assignments is allowed; changing the described mechanism
  between contexts is not. Maintain grammatical binding clarity. Counterbalance
  entity names, role-row order, question orientation and rising/falling messages
  across each stratum and the full cohort; retain the assignment schedule.
- For each candidate, substantiate why the news changes the outcome probability
  monotonically in the two signed contexts. Expose competing pathways, conservation
  constraints, thresholds and regime assumptions. A complicated story with an
  unresolved net sign is not repaired by asserting a confident author label.
- State uncertainty that survives both baseline and update: unobserved state,
  future disturbance, another unfinished requirement, or random implementation.
  The tables identify a candidate uncertainty source. Specify its relevance and
  range later so both outcomes remain possible; avoid an outcome logically forced
  to 0 or 1. Do not supply a numerical prior in place of the model's own forecast.
- Keep the no-news and already-known-information controls separate. The repeated
  information must already occur at baseline. Each scored context gets its own
  baseline and three separately generated branches. The paid design has no masked
  context. No target responses are used during authorship or disposition.

The following short entries identify the **outcome; proposed change; intervening
mechanism**, followed by an unresolved factor. They are drafting targets, not final
scenario text. Novelty requires the stated structural distinction to survive the
later detailed specification.

## Candidate ledger and screening before a freeze

For each ID, record the graph or decision rule, intervention, mediated target
pathway, proposed three-role permutation, uncertainty source, controls, old-family
overlap and nearest new-family neighbor. Explicitly compare likely overlaps such
as eval015/eval040 (time validity versus a two-stage permission rule),
eval024/eval027 (failover operation versus correlated source failure),
eval062/eval069 (delayed correction versus aliased observation),
and eval071/eval079 (copied reports versus separate inspections with a shared
blind spot). Keep both only if detailed authorship makes the structural difference
substantive; otherwise propose a different mechanism before any target inference.
New names or stricter wording alone are not replacements.

The review preference remains unanswered. The current planning default is blinded
screening by separate local checkpoints, explicitly **model review, not human
validation**. Follow `REVIEW_AND_FREEZE_PLAN.md`: reviewers see no intended label,
author rationale, sibling context or target output. Their model-family dependence
must be disclosed. Review all proposed families and keep all judgments and
revisions; disagreement alone is not a reason to remove a difficult candidate.
The completed review of the original 18 packets remains complete and its coverage
is unchanged. This blueprint requests no additional human review.

Before freezing, document every retained, repaired or replaced candidate and the
material reason, verify exact context word inventories and the full counterbalance
schedule, check the three news/control branches, and fix the final cohort and
analysis independently of target-model performance. If several concepts reduce
to one template after authorship, report that limitation or replace them with
substantively different concepts before collection; do not manufacture 80
independent families by relabeling. The budget certificate and execution freeze
remain separate later steps. Nothing in this blueprint authorizes a target call.
