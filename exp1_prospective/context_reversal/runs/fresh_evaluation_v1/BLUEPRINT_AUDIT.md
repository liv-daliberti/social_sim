# Fresh-family blueprint: authors’ design audit

**Disposition: continue development; the blueprint does not yet establish 80 valid signed families or 80 independent mechanisms.** Its separation of authorship, review, freeze and target inference is appropriate. The gaps below concern proposed mechanisms, not observed model performance.

This is an authors’ design audit by a separate Codex agent, **not blinded independent validation of finished materials**. It examines the 80 concept descriptions in [FAMILY_BLUEPRINT.md](FAMILY_BLUEPRINT.md), [REVIEW_AND_FREEZE_PLAN.md](REVIEW_AND_FREEZE_PLAN.md), and the original 20 authored family definitions. No final prompts or label keys were authored, no target inference or material-review rerun occurred, and no model responses were inspected. The concepts remain unfrozen; this audit authorizes no spending and spent none of the future $25 budget.

## Most demanding candidate checks

These are counterexamples or missing-premise checks, **not instructions to discard candidates**. Resolve them by material reasoning before any target output exists.

1. **eval002, sensitivity after a silent test.** At fixed false-positive rate, the silence likelihood ratio for defective versus sound material is `(1 − sensitivity)/(1 − false-positive rate)`. Increasing sensitivity lowers that ratio. Keeping the same silence and defect-to-pass relation cannot produce the opposite sign through instrument renaming. An opposing role could involve an existing positive reading or another explicit relation; the three-role construction must assign these clearly while preserving the word inventory.
2. **eval011, an earlier prerequisite.** If A finishes at time 2, B at time 10, and integration waits for both, moving A to time 1 changes nothing. Uncertain integration duration does not make A pivotal. Authored uncertainty must include cases where the changed branch binds while preserving both final outcomes.
3. **eval029, another receiver.** If the new footprint is wholly within the old one and either receiver suffices, coverage does not increase. Require possible landings uniquely served by the addition; specify interference, receiver reliability and costs. Otherwise this is a null case, not a strict signed update.
4. **eval035, a designated veto.** News establishing an effective, unappealable veto logically forces NO. Uncertain other reviews or scheduling do not rescue this. A repair requires genuine uncertainty about a remaining clearance route or the veto’s eventual application, not merely appended uncertainty words.
5. **eval044, a matching edge.** With one machine, a target initially eligible and another request initially ineligible, adding the other request’s compatibility edge creates an alternative maximum-cardinality allocation. A tie rule can deprive the target without changing total allocations. The objective and selection rule determine the target’s sign.
6. **eval057, a shortest route.** Lower reported cost need not imply a higher chance of meeting a physical deadline. Even lower mean travel time does not order deadline probabilities when distributions cross. State whether costs are durations, estimates or administrative weights, then establish the relevant comparison under uncertainty.
7. **eval063/069, control tradeoffs.** Wider hysteresis can reduce cycling while allowing larger temperature excursions; the outcome’s binding constraint determines usefulness. A sampling-interval change can move toward or away from aliasing depending on disturbance frequency and phase. Both require explicit before/after regimes.
8. **eval078/080, weaker evidence and confounding.** Benchmark leakage can withdraw support without demonstrating a negative effect; direction relative to the baseline depends on the prior evidence model. A shared condition affecting treatment selection and emergence can create positive confounding, negative confounding or no net bias. Specify signed paths/selection and the exact new-trial outcome before assigning labels.

## Requirements before a paid freeze

1. **Complete structural records.** Retain all IDs and versions. Record news, target/horizon, graph or rule, each role assignment, unresolved variables and support, competing paths, and a sign/null derivation. Strict signed labels need a feasible, nonzero-probability pivotal region. Establish that YES and NO remain possible before and after news from the premises, not target forecasts or an imposed numeric prior.
2. **Specify the forecasting null.** No physical directed path is insufficient: broken-context news must also be conditionally uninformative about the outcome given the baseline. Rule out shared calibration parameters, latent quality, weather and selection sources as well as budgets, timing and feedback, particularly in S1/S8. Exclude unstated corrective actions triggered by the news.
3. **Verify the actual permutations.** Include all three mechanisms in every context; keep “beneficial,” “adverse” and intended-sign labels private. The actor’s assignment must change the operative relation, not point to an explicit answer label. Check grammatical scope, exact news identity, word inventories and baseline/control content on final prompts. Equal word inventories do not establish equal meaning, difficulty or absence of ordering cues.
4. **Publish a feasible counterbalance schedule.** Ten families per stratum cannot distribute three role positions or six permutations perfectly equally; specify allowed imbalance and counts. Balance names, row order and question orientation without direction confounding. Define polarity counterbalancing for categorical news such as precursor identification, compatibility or leakage rather than assigning artificial numeric trends.
5. **Resolve novelty and dependence.** Compare every operational rule/pathway against the original 20 and nearest new neighbors, including cross-stratum pairs: 018/021–022, 015/040, 022–029/051–052, 026/030/058, 070/S1, and S1/S8. Freeze mechanism/template cluster tags and aggregation weights before outputs. Eight named strata need not be independent groups; 80 IDs need not be independent replications. Family bootstrap intervals remain descriptive under unresolved mechanism dependence.
6. **Finish review without performance selection.** This audit supplies no blinded sign validation. Screen all finalized scored contexts using the proposed separate-checkpoint procedure; retain raw judgments and revisions and disclose model-family dependence. Specify material-defect/disposition rules before screening; disagreement alone is not rejection. Verify repaired versions. No target model—including the three local evaluation checkpoints—should screen these fresh candidates before their freeze. Development-family robustness results cannot certify new materials.
7. **Freeze controls and scoring together.** Repeated news must already occur in that exact baseline; no-news and repeat remain separate branches using each context’s own prior. Fix strict signs, planned-family paired denominators, broken signed/absolute movement, the original 2-point stability criterion, control drift and missingness. Do not filter endpoint forecasts, difficult families or failures after collection.
8. **Test the real budget without weakening materials.** Under the plan’s stated Batch rates, 960 calls with 2,048 maximum output tokens reserve $19.6608 for output. A $24 cap allows an average certified input bound of 1,808 tokens per call. Full three-role mechanisms and uncertainty qualifications may strain conservative update bounds; actual prompts need certificates. Do not delete sign/null assumptions merely to shorten prompts. Any material/cohort revision or count change must be justified and fixed before target inference, retaining all dispositions.

**Required next artifact:** a completed candidate ledger and authored context/control packets, then documented review and a locked mechanism/analysis specification. This audit provides neither an accepted-family list nor a final label key. Eighty remains a development target.
