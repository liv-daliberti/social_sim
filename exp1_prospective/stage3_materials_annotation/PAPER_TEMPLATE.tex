\subsubsection{Human Validation of Stage~3 Evidence}
\label{app:exp1-stage3-human-validation}

\paragraph{Annotator pool and consent.}
We used eight volunteer adult annotators, recruited without compensation from the
authors' academic networks. Annotators gave informed consent on the task page and
could withdraw at any time. The form requested no sensitive or identifying
personal information.

\paragraph{Procedure.}
Each annotator independently rated the same 18 synthetic evidence packets, drawn
deterministically from 18 distinct markets and balanced across pro-YES, anti-YES,
and orthogonal directions. Before each synthetic report, the form reproduced the
matching summary from the frozen June 10 initial forecast, so annotators and
models conditioned on the same pre-report information. Source URLs, intended
labels, counterfactual-generator rationales, and model responses were withheld.
Annotators judged conditional direction and confidence, clarity, plausibility,
and whether each report was usable as an explicit hypothetical premise. Item
order was independently randomized. A self-review pilot led us to remove three
non-gating questions and add the pre-report summaries before registered
collection; the final five-judgment form took approximately 15--20 minutes.
The consent screen and every item instructed annotators to treat June 10, 2026
as the current date and not use subsequently learned events or outcomes, matching
the temporal information available to the models.

\paragraph{IRB status.}
This activity consisted solely of judgments about synthetic, model-generated text
and did not collect sensitive or identifying data or intervene on participants.
Responses were used to characterize the materials rather than the annotators.
Under our institutional guidelines, the activity did not constitute
human-subjects research; consequently, no IRB review was sought.
