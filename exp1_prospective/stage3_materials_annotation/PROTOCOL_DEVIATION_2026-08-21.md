# Post-collection deviation: directional straight-lining exclusion

Decision time: 2026-08-21T13:21:30Z

This document supplements, but does not rewrite, the frozen
`stage3_materials_annotation_v6` protocol. The frozen protocol specified that no
annotator would be excluded after inspection. The decision below is therefore a
post-collection deviation and must be described as such wherever the filtered
analysis is reported.

## Roster extensions

The original version 6 protocol contained seven reviewers. Versions 7 and 8,
created after interim responses had been inspected, added independently ordered
assignments for `annotator_08` and `annotator_09`. The 18 items, scoring key, and
all earlier reviewer assignments were unchanged. Analyses using the expanded
nine-reviewer roster are therefore labeled sensitivity extensions; they do not
replace the original seven-reviewer specification.

## Directional straight-lining rule

After four reviewers had completed the packet, `annotator_02` had selected
`more_likely` on 17 of 18 items. The packet was balanced by construction: six
items had registered direction `more_likely`, six `less_likely`, and six
`no_material_effect`. The reviewer selected `more_likely` for all six registered
negative items and five of six registered null items. The authors classified
this response pattern as a failure to perform the requested conditional-direction
task.

After the rule had been adopted, and after the reviewer had been told their score
and 17-of-18 response pattern, the researcher conducted a follow-up debrief. The
reviewer reported interpreting the task as judging whether reports were convincing
in favor of YES, difficulty with English and market-specific terminology (including
interpreting "knockout stage" as failure to advance), consulting a dictionary and
an AI assistant despite the no-outside-assistance instruction, and devoting less
attention than intended. No re-rating was accepted. These statements are recorded
as context for the observed response pattern, not as an independent exclusion
criterion: the debrief was not blinded, followed performance feedback, and included
researcher-provided examples. The exclusion rests only on the uniformly applied
response-pattern rule below.

The quality rule is: exclude any completed registered reviewer who selects one
conditional-direction option on at least 17 of 18 items. The rule is applied
identically to all reviewers. Raw responses are retained unchanged. No item is
removed or relabeled.

The quality-filtered analysis preserves the frozen gate's allowance for at most
one dissenting reviewer. A complete nine-reviewer extension with one excluded
reviewer therefore requires seven of eight quality-eligible judgments for the
direction and premise-usability gates. Interim completed-panel snapshots apply
the same one-dissent rule to the available complete packets (currently five of
six). An all-completed sensitivity analysis retains the excluded reviewer. Every
report must disclose that both the exclusion rule and roster extensions followed
inspection of interim responses.
